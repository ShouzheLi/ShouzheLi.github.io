Making simulation scores comparable.

A reproducible evaluation stack for VLA models: scene onboarding, camera and rendering fidelity, tiered protocols, and an audit of the ways a score can silently stop meaning anything.

  • RoleSimulation & evaluation
  • StackIsaac Sim · Isaac Lab · Arena
  • StatusPrivate platform · abstracted
中文摘要 面向 VLA 的可复现仿真评测栈。核心不是排行榜，而是判断一个分数是否可比：场景、相机域、渲染管线、协议状态都是量测的一部分。私有平台，细节抽象，证据条目见下方链接。

01Problem

Simulation scores are usually read as model properties. In practice they are properties of the whole measurement stack: renderer, camera domain, criteria, protocol state, and data path. A single changed setting can move success rate by tens of points.

That makes two failures common: a number that looks right but is not comparable, and a pipeline that fails silently while still producing a plausible leaderboard.

02Approach

01 / SCENE

Lock the world.

Assets, poses, physics, cameras and reset events are declared once, then held by contract tests before any score exists.

02 / PROTOCOL

Declare the claim.

Conditions are chosen before validation and read-only during it. Protocol state decides whether a run can enter a leaderboard.

03 / RECEIPT

Keep the trace.

Keyframes, state, per-episode results, and a gate that can say practice or not validated. A score without evidence is a story.

03Evidence

  • 01Renderer and camera domain move the score by 55 points2026-10-16
  • 02Scene onboarding locked by contract tests2026-10-16
  • 03Starting the engine without renderer injection froze the host2026-09-15

Entries are dated, carry a condition set and a boundary. The first is measured; the other two are documented incidents.

04Boundary

  • —Private platform: no public benchmark, no external leaderboard, details abstracted.
  • —The 30 to 85 result proves measurement sensitivity, not a model improvement.
  • —No dyno-grade actuator identification; no claim that Newton runs the full robot in production.
