49 interactive assets, from video only.

A Real2Sim pass that treats footage as the only source of truth: appearance, geometry and interaction, rebuilt as USD and replayed in Newton.

中文摘要以实拍视频为唯一依据，重建 49 项可交互 USD 资产（7 个物体类目 + 4 处室内环境），在 Newton 中复现真实交互；输出逐视角外观对照与上下并置的交互对比视频。

01Problem

There is no CAD and no scan: a short capture is all you get. The asset still has to be aligned, physically plausible and visually comparable at every viewpoint.

02Approach

01 / SOLVE

Fix the views.

Camera poses and scale first; geometry before materials. A wrong pose makes every later check meaningless.

02 / BUILD

Build for contact.

Collision proxies, mass and contact offsets, not just a render mesh. The asset has to survive the first touch.

03 / REPLAY

Replay in Newton.

Run the same interaction in simulation and compare per view, then cut the paired clips.

03Result

49

Interactive assets

USD assets with collision and material, usable in Newton.

Categories

Comparison modes

Per-view appearance stills and stacked interaction clips.
