Method note · 2026

Evidence before geometry.

A Real2Sim procedure for turning a short capture into an asset that is aligned, physically plausible and comparable — without pretending a mesh is a measurement.

  • RoleReal2Sim & asset pipeline
  • StackUSD · ICP · reconstruction
  • StatusMethod note · no accepted asset yet
中文摘要 这是一份方法说明，不是已验收的资产。原则是先把证据分层——身份、接口、轮廓、接触——再动几何；ICP 只在粗对齐后做局部精配准，不能替代识别与尺度恢复。

01Problem

The bottleneck is not generating a pretty mesh. It is producing an asset whose identity, interfaces and contact behaviour can be defended. A model that looks right from one angle can still fail the first contact.

CAD and footage disagree more often than expected: CAD proves design data, not the revision on the bench; video constrains silhouette, not hidden structure.

02Approach

01 / IDENTITY

Confirm the part.

Model, revision and assembly are checked before any dimension is trusted. A CAD file is evidence of design intent, not of the object on the table.

02 / INTERFACE

Measure what mates.

Mounts, openings and clearances come from measured geometry; the observable silhouette comes from multi-view footage.

03 / CONTACT

Then proxy and test.

Collision proxies are built to measured size, with contact offsets checked. ICP only refines a coarse pose; it does not recognize objects or recover scale.
