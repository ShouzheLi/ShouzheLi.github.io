System · 2026

A knowledge system that leaves a trail.

Two skills and one router: broad embodied AI expertise, deep platform knowledge, and an update rule that keeps both honest.

01Problem

Most AI knowledge tools are static notes. They age quietly: a claim that was true in March still reads as true in November, and there is no record of what was overturned.

The second problem is granularity. Broad expertise and platform-specific traps have different update rates. Mixing them makes triggers blunt and maintenance expensive.

02Approach

01 / SPLIT

Two skills, one router.

A broad embodied AI skill and a deep simulation-platform skill. The agent decides which to load; knowledge is not duplicated.

02 / GRADE

Evidence has a status.

Claims are separated into fact, judgment and recommendation, with a status and a date. Unverified notes are marked, not smoothed over.

03 / WRITE BACK

Every project returns.

New lessons are written into the skill with a source and a boundary. The site radar is the same loop, pointed outward.

04Boundary

  • —No benchmark of answer quality; this is a working system, not a product claim.
  • —The 248-source figure is a dated baseline, not a live count.
  • —Automation handles formatting and checks; judgment and evidence grading stay manual.
