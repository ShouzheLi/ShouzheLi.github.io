Simulation → policy → data.

An embodied manipulation platform that produces both policies and synthetic episodes from one simulation stack.

  • RoleSimulation platform & RL
  • StackIsaac Sim · Isaac Lab · PPO · LeRobot · Ray Tune

中文摘要仿真→策略→数据全链路；绝缘子抓取 93%、放置 82%，合成数据 100 条以上。

01Problem

Policies and data are usually produced by separate teams and separate pipelines. Here both had to come out of the same environment.

02Approach

01 / ENV

Build the task.

Insulator grasp and place environments with a fixed success definition.

02 / TRAIN

Train the policy.

PPO in Isaac Lab with reward shaping and parallel rollouts.

03 / EXPORT

Export the data.

Rollouts become synthetic episodes through LeRobot for downstream training.

04Boundary

  • —Success rates are self-reported and task-specific.
  • —No public protocol, seed set or held-out site.
  • —Not a general manipulation claim.
  • —Client and site details are abstracted.
