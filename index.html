How I work

Across the whole loop, with a measurement-first bias.

The last ten percent is not a demo — it is the measurement, the failure mode, and the evidence.

01 / MEASUREMENT

If you can't reproduce it, it isn't a result.

Every claim needs n, a confidence interval, a failure taxonomy, and a traceable artifact. A number without evidence is a story.

02 / SIMULATION

Simulation is an instrument.

Rendering, camera, physics, and protocol are part of the number. I calibrate the stack before I compare the model.

03 / DEPLOYMENT

Deployment is the test.

MTBF, intervention rate, and unit economics decide whether the loop is real. A demo is not a deployment.
